## Synopsis
K-Pop: Demon Hunters follows a group of world-famous K-pop idols who secretly live double lives as demon hunters. By day, they perform on global stages, dazzling fans with music and dance — but by night, they battle dark supernatural forces threatening humanity. The film combines stylish action sequences, emotional storytelling, and K-pop energy.
